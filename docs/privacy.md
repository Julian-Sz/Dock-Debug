# Dock-Debug Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 30, 2026*

Dock-Debug does not collect, transmit or sell any data. The app has no network access, no account and no
analytics or telemetry.

## What the app stores on your computer

When you switch on tracking, Dock-Debug writes log files to your own computer, in
`Documents\Dock-Debug\tracking`. They contain information about your hardware and system:

- names, hardware IDs and serial numbers of monitors, docks and USB devices, and of the devices
  connected through them;
- driver and firmware versions, USB connection speeds and device status;
- your computer model, BIOS and Windows version, power settings and battery state;
- Windows event log entries about devices, displays, power, hardware errors, crashes and driver
  updates;
- the notes you type into problem markers.

## Who can see it

These files never leave your computer unless you share them yourself, for example with an AI assistant
of your choice. Dock-Debug does not send them anywhere.

## Deleting the data

You can delete the log files at any time. Uninstalling the app does not delete them; delete the folder
`Documents\Dock-Debug` yourself if you want to remove them.
